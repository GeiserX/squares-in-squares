## The tilted-band generator: what it found (2026-11-07)

The large-n packings really are this structure. Our n=498 packing (side 22.8231) is 233 upright squares on a grid anchored at the bottom-right corner, 211 on one anchored at the top-left, and 54 tilted squares on the corner-to-corner diagonal between them. The tilted squares sit at about 25 degrees (36 of them at 25.3), not at the band's 45. | n | side | most squares a band layout holds | needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 498 | 22.8231 (ours) | 492 | 498 |
| 498 | 22.7939 (leader) | 492 | 498 |
| 593 | 24.8467 (ours) | 589 | 593 |

Versions tried, in order: band squares aligned with the band (488 at n=498), a sheared tilted lattice cut to a strip (490), and the channel greedy fill (492). The real channels hold squares at several angles, relaxed against each other, which a rigid greedy fill does not reproduce.
